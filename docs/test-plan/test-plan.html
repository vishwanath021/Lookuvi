Executed on 24 October 2026 against the local server. AI provider is mock. WhatsApp is not a live handset: webhook signatures use a local secret, and reminder sends use an injected sender that never calls Meta. `OPENAI_API_KEY` was removed from the process. Demo logins only.

193 pass, 0 fail, 4 untested, 197 cases.

Onboarding checklist

StepDo thisWhat good looks like
1Super admin creates the salonName, lowercase slug, city, WhatsApp, owner email, a password of at least 12 characters that is not a demo password. Plan as sold. Queue is already on. One trial grant. Styles are copied.
2Owner signs in and finishes onboardingBrand, services, WhatsApp, languages, then Finish. Staff can see the wizard until Finish.
3Owner sets chairs and hoursOn the queue desk, not in Settings. Chairs 1–30, average minutes, live line or time requests, chair/station/room. Pause the line before the QR is public if the shop is not ready.
4Owner checks capsAnonymous daily, member daily, monthly try-ons, visit tries for bridal. Daily and monthly reset at midnight Asia/Kolkata.
5Owner confirms remindersOn by default, 35 days, range 28–42. WhatsApp monthly cap default 50. Reminders and offers count. Queue alerts and the desk WhatsApp button do not.
6Print the QR only on the real domainThe code points at the welcome page. Localhost refuses to print and says it is not ready.
7Staff rehearsalJoin from a phone, Next, Start, Done. In request mode: confirm, decline, Mark visited. Walk-in only on the live line.
8Book this lookA guest tap creates a lead and opens WhatsApp. Lite does not download the CSV. Starter and Pro do.
9STOP rehearsalA reply of STOP opts out that salon only. STOPALL or UNSUBSCRIBE opts out the shared number everywhere. The owner cannot undo STOP.
10Leave billing, bookings, guest hub, custom domain, outlets, salon AI key, embed, and the guide offTell the owner those are not in this launch.
